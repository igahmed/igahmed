## About Me

I'm Cybersecurity undergraduate at **Dawood University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), Pakistan**, focused on building intelligent security systems and advancing modern Security Operations.

My technical interests center around **Security Operations (SOC), Detection Engineering, Threat Hunting, SIEM, SOAR, and AI-driven Cybersecurity**.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity — exploring how Machine Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Explainable AI (XAI) can improve threat detection, security investigations, and incident response.

Beyond practical defensive security, I actively explore emerging security challenges involving autonomous AI agents, identity, access management, and dynamic authorization.
